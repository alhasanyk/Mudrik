Algorithmic Integrity & Mathematical Note

Results above are directly derived from the simulated Jeddah test coordinates. Distance calculations utilize the Euclidean metric factored by 1.25 to reflect street grid curvature. Diesel consumption is evaluated at 38 L/100 km for a standard municipal 3-axle compactor. Financial and environmental figures represent calculated prototype outputs under simulated parameters and do not constitute real audit claims.

Payload Weight Measurement Methodology Distinction

Important engineering caveat: Individual container weights shown below are estimated by multiplying the ultrasonic volume displacement (m³) by typical municipal solid waste density (~280 kg/m³ for uncompacted urban residential refuse). Only total cumulative trip weight is measured via the collection truck’s onboard axle load-cells at the depot weighbridge. Individual container weights cannot be treated as certified scale measurements.

MUDRIK System Architecture & Algorithmic Specifications

This section details the mathematical formulas, hardware IoT topologies, and routing heuristics used to demonstrate MUDRIK at the smart-cities hackathon.

1. Mathematical Evaluation Formulas

Distance Reduction (%)
= ((Baseline Distance - Optimized Distance) / Baseline Distance) * 100
Estimated Fuel Consumed (L)
= Route Distance (km) * (Vehicle Consumption Rate [38 L/100km]) / 100
Priority Urgency Score (P_i)
= (Fill% * 1.5) + (HoursSinceLastCollection * 0.4) + (CriticalRiskFactor * 25)

Assumptions: Fuel cost indexed at SAR 1.15 per liter of municipal diesel. Vehicle consumption rate is fixed at 38 L/100km representing standard 20m³ hydraulic compactors.
